When specifying `size`, the suffixes `k`, `m`, `g` and `t` are now interpreted as decimal (powers of 1000), and `KiB`, `MiB`, `GiB` and `TiB` are binary (powers of 1024). The `b`, `p` and `e` suffixes are no longer supported. Previously, `size` values were interpreted as 1024-based values, so the same size definitions will produce a slightly smaller image.
